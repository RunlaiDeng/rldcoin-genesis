# Automatic mining and future regional payments

The product goal is peer-to-peer payments between future human communities across star systems.

| Stage | Status |
|---|---|
| A Permanent identity and recoverable operation | Original Earth genesis retained; legacy operating roles retired at a verified terminal checkpoint. |
| B PoW consensus and accounting | Released: authentic work, exact supply, maturity, signed local transfers, fees and greater-work reorganizations. |
| C Node and automatic miner | Released: durable replay, bounded APIs, peer synchronization, automatic block announcements and actual hashing telemetry. |
| D Explicit rule adoption and operation | Activated: original history replay, unanimous same-controller signatures, real mined rewards, restart and Mac revalidation. |
| E Usable local payments | In progress: developer transfer API and process tests exist; consumer wallet, convenient offline signing, a production mature-value payment and a separately qualified seconds-scale local payment layer remain. |
| F Asynchronous regional settlement | Future qualification: source locking, authenticated checkpoints, unique import, delayed receipts, deep reorganizations and disconnected delivery. |
| G Open and lasting operation | Independent participants and audits, broader mining distribution, authenticated retention and upgrades, eventual real distant deployment. |

A new region cannot duplicate the reserve. All regions must share the same fixed total supply. Cross-region routes and issuance-budget transfers remain disabled until separately specified and verified. Proof of work does not remove communication delay or provide irreversible finality.

The current node accepts a signed transaction as pending (`confirmed: false`) before a block exists. Ten minutes is the PoW target interval, not a fixed transaction time or a completed payment. The local payment experience target is a verifiable, enforceable receipt within three seconds at P95 when both parties are online and a noncustodial payment channel is already funded. This target is not implemented; channel funding and settlement still need blocks, and interstellar communication cannot be instantaneous.
