What this launch establishes

A permanent identity, recoverable history and a public verification path. One owner currently controls the operating roles and both recovery hosts. Agent technical review and actual recovery checks have been performed; independent security review, hardware custody and independent operators are future requirements.

RLD is intended to be earned through verified protocol services. Admission computation alone does not issue coins. The founder’s goal of earning at least 5% before promotion is an operational goal, with no reserved share or guaranteed return.

This launch does not establish operational interstellar payments. Value activation and reward issuance require their later protocol and safety conditions. The signing exception expires at the date above and does not renew automatically.
